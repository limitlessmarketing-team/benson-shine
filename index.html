FAQ

Quick answers.

How often should I have my windows cleaned?

Most homes in Northern Utah look best with a clean in spring and again in fall, after the dust and sprinkler season. Storefronts usually go monthly or every other week, since clean glass is part of the first impression.

Do I need to be home?

Not for an exterior-only clean, as long as we can get to the windows and any gates are unlocked. For interior work someone will need to let us in.

Can you get hard water spots off?

In most cases, yes. Sprinkler and hard water spots take extra treatment beyond a standard wash, so let us know when you request a quote and we'll plan for it. Heavily etched glass may not come back to 100%, and we'll tell you that up front.

What about screens, tracks and sills?

We clean all of it. Screens come off and get washed, tracks get vacuumed and scrubbed, sills get wiped inside and out. Just check the boxes on the quote form.

Do you do commercial and storefront windows?

Yes. Barbershops, restaurants, offices, retail. We can come before you open or after you close and set up a recurring schedule so you never have to think about it.

How do you price a job?

By the job, not the hour. We'll ask how many windows, how many stories, and whether you want inside and out. From there you get one number, and that's the number you pay.
